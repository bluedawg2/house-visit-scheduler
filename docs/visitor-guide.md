# Visitor Guide -- House Visit Scheduler

## How to Book a Visit

Booking a visit takes 3 simple steps. You'll be guided through each one.

---

### Step 1: Select Your Dates

When you open the booking page, you'll see a calendar and two date fields.

- **Calendar**: Green highlighted dates are available for booking. If a date is not green, it is not available.
- **Check-in**: Pick the date you want to arrive.
- **Check-out**: Pick the date you want to leave.

Once you select valid dates, the page will confirm how many nights your stay is. If there's an issue (dates not available, overlap with another booking, or stay length outside the allowed range), you'll see a message explaining what to fix.

When your dates look good, click **"Next: Your Details"** to continue.

---

### Step 2: Enter Your Details

You'll see a summary of your selected dates at the top. If you need to change them, click **"Back to dates"**.

Fill in the form:

- **Your Name** (required)
- **Your Email** (required) -- this is where you'll receive updates about your request
- **Anything we should know?** (optional) -- number of guests, arrival time, special requests, etc.

Click **"Submit Request"** when you're ready.

---

### Step 3: Confirmation

You'll see a confirmation screen with your booking details (check-in, check-out, number of nights).

**What happens next:**

- The host will review your request
- You'll receive an email when your request is accepted or declined
- If accepted, the email will include a calendar invite you can add to your calendar

If you'd like to book another visit, click **"Book another stay"**.

---

## Checking Your Request Status

At the bottom of the booking page, you can check the status of any request you've submitted.

1. Scroll down to **"Check Your Request Status"**
2. Enter the email address you used when booking
3. Click **"Look Up"**

You'll see all your requests with their current status:

- **Pending** -- The host hasn't reviewed it yet
- **Accepted** -- You're confirmed! Check your email for details
- **Rejected** -- The host was unable to accommodate this request. You can try different dates

If the host added a note (for example, a reason for declining), it will appear below the status.

---

## Tips

- Only dates highlighted in green on the calendar are available -- don't try to book dates that aren't green
- Make sure your email address is correct so you receive updates
- If your request is rejected, you're welcome to submit a new request for different dates
